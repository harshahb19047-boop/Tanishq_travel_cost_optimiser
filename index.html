Air India premium analysis — cost of not switching
DEL-BLR route: Air India avg ₹10,142 vs IndiGo ₹7,397 — premium of ₹2,745/ticket. With 55 Air India trips, excess spend = ₹1.51 L on this route alone.
BLR-DEL route: Air India avg ₹8,424 vs IndiGo ₹7,588 — premium of ₹836/ticket. With 91 Air India trips, excess = ₹76K.
SpiceJet anomaly: BOM-BLR SpiceJet avg ₹20,940 — nearly 3× IndiGo rate. 13 trips = ₹1.97 L excess. Likely includes excess charges or last-minute fares.
BLR-BOM: Surprisingly, Air India (₹6,730) is cheaper than IndiGo (₹6,277) by only ₹453 — the smallest premium, meaning this route is not where to focus airline switch efforts.

Phase 1 — Quick wins (months 1–2)
1

Enforce advance booking via portal block

Configure the travel portal to block air ticket approvals booked less than 5 days before travel. All exceptions require DM+ written override. Policy already mandates 7-day advance — this just adds a system enforcement layer.

Est. savings: ₹50–75 L/yr · Complexity: Low
2

IndiGo-first carrier rule with premium alert

Portal shows lowest-cost option first. If employee selects Air India where IndiGo operates the same route, a pop-up appears requiring manager justification. Focus: DEL-BLR, BLR-DEL where AI premium is ₹836–2,745/ticket.

Est. savings: ₹40–60 L/yr · Complexity: Low
3

Non-FCM hotel audit & mandatory portal rule

Cross-check all non-portal hotel claims against Table 4 city limits. Recover overages. Make portal mandatory for hotel bookings above ₹3,000/night. Out-of-portal claims reimbursed at 80% of eligible limit as policy reminder.

Est. savings: ₹40–60 L/yr · Complexity: Low
4

BLR-BOM group travel coordination

Central Travel Desk sends weekly route-overlap digest for top 5 routes. Employees on same route within 2 days are flagged to coordinate. Policy already mandates Central Desk for 9+ persons — just need detection.

Est. savings: ₹20–35 L/yr · Complexity: Low
Phase 2 — Medium-term (months 3–6)
5

Route-based fare baselines & budget alerts

Calculate 25th-percentile fare for each of the top 20 routes from FCM data. Portal alerts when selected fare exceeds baseline by 20%. Updated quarterly. Converts this dashboard's route data into a live control tool.

Est. savings: ₹40–60 L/yr · Complexity: Medium
6

30-day repeat-visit approval escalation

Same employee to same city within 30 days triggers one level higher approval than standard. Forces manager to actively justify every trip. Reduces habit travel without blocking legitimate business needs.

Est. savings: ₹15–30 L/yr · Complexity: Low
7

Boarding limit compliance tracker

Monthly report mapping each employee's boarding claims vs their level entitlement (₹750–₹2,000/day per policy Table 5). Share with cost owners quarterly. Awareness alone reduces over-claiming by 8–12%.

Est. savings: ₹10–20 L/yr · Complexity: Low
8

Cost owner accountability dashboard

Convert this analysis into a live monthly report shared with each cost owner showing their spend per trip vs company average. No targets initially — data visibility alone shifts behaviour. Set targets in month 3.

Est. savings: ₹20–30 L/yr through accountability · Complexity: Low
